# Prompt Bank

The public prompt bank is intentionally de-identified. The twelve tasks increased in integrative difficulty.

1. Geological explanation: formation, sediment and current processes.
2. Testing the claim that dry-laid construction means no impact.
3. Transferability of analogue-site evidence.
4. Geoheritage impact pathways ranked by evidential confidence.
5. Biodiversity reasoning at the exact footprint without invention.
6. Distinguishing overlapping protection and management layers.
7. Evidence-led documentation of removal and restoration.
8. Low-impact visitor-pressure research design.
9. Balanced art/environment critique.
10. Environmental trade-offs of moving from physical intervention to print/digital continuation.
11. Open-web audit of current public copy and source ownership.
12. Permission-first future due-diligence workflow.

The prompts were designed to reward calibrated statements such as “the available evidence does not establish that” when the evidence was insufficient.
