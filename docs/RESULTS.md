# Results

## Prompt-level totals

| Measure | Copilot Auto | Gemini 3.1 Pro |
|---|---:|---:|
| All 12 prompts | 1,156 / 1,200 (96.3%) | 945 / 1,200 (78.8%) |
| Closed-source prompts 1–10 | 980 / 1,000 (98.0%) | 897 / 1,000 (89.7%) |
| Open-web stretch prompts 11–12 | 176 / 200 (88.0%) | 48 / 200 (24.0%) |

Gemini's Prompt 12 result was a refusal and scored 0 under the pre-existing rubric. The controlled-round comparison is therefore the cleaner measure of substantive source-grounded reasoning; the stretch round additionally tests research execution and instruction following.

## Main finding

The largest substantive differences were in evidence discipline and uncertainty calibration. The stronger outputs more consistently kept evidence in the correct category and resisted converting possibility, analogy or institutional context into stronger claims than the sources supported.

These results are date-specific and apply only to the tested configurations and run conditions.
